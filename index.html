Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required to rent a car?

Please contact Linkedinn directly on 024 265 0673 to confirm the exact documents, minimum age and driving-experience requirements for your rental. These details are confirmed by the team at the time of enquiry.

Is a deposit required?

Deposit requirements and any refund terms are confirmed by the Linkedinn team during your enquiry. Please ask when you contact us so the current policy is explained clearly.

What is the fuel policy?

Fuel arrangements are confirmed with you before the rental begins. Please ask the team about the fuel level at pickup and the expected return level when you enquire.

Self-drive or with a driver?

Whether a vehicle is offered self-drive or with a driver depends on the vehicle and your requirements. Please confirm which option you need when you enquire, and the team will advise on availability.

Do you offer airport or out-of-town pickup?

Linkedinn is based at Eastern By-pass, Airport Roundabout, Kumasi. For airport or out-of-town pickup, please contact the team on 024 265 0673 to confirm where and how pickup can be arranged.

What payment options are available?

Accepted payment methods and any payment terms are confirmed by the Linkedinn team during your enquiry. Please ask when you contact us.

What if I need to return the vehicle later than agreed?

If you expect to return the vehicle later than agreed, please contact Linkedinn as early as possible on 024 265 0673 so the team can advise on any extension and applicable terms.
