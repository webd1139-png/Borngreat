Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required to rent a car?

A valid driver's licence, national or international, and a means of identification (national ID, passport, or voter's card). Minimum age is 21 years with at least 2 years of driving experience.

Is a deposit required?

Yes, a refundable deposit is required. The amount varies by vehicle category and will be confirmed during the booking enquiry. The deposit is refunded upon safe return of the vehicle.

What is the fuel policy?

Rentals are provided with a full tank of fuel. The vehicle should be returned with a full tank. If returned empty, a refuelling charge plus service fee will apply.

Self-drive or with a driver?

Both options are available. Self-drive rentals are ideal for experienced drivers who want independence. With-driver options include a professional driver and are recommended for visitors or those who prefer not to drive.

Airport pickup available?

Yes! We offer airport pickup at Kotoka International Airport (Accra) and any other Ghanaian airport. The meeting point is the Airport Roundabout in Kumasi for local pickups. Please provide your flight details during enquiry.

Payment options - MoMo, cash?

We accept Mobile Money (MoMo), bank transfer, and cash payments. Full payment or deposit terms are confirmed during the booking enquiry.

What happens if I return the car late?

A grace period of 30 minutes is provided. After 30 minutes, a late fee applies (typically 20% of the daily rate per hour). Please contact us immediately if you expect to be late.
